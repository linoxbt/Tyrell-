## Against the spec's estimate

Section 12 estimated 90–130k gas per `anchor`. A successful single anchor costs about 95k (`test_anchorHappyPath`). On a deployment linked to its agent (`MemoryAnchor.linkAgent`), an agent-lane anchor also checks the agent live in `AgentRegistry`, including the QIE ID lookup, and costs up to about 145k (the max column). A full `anchorBatch` of 20 costs about 1.33M, roughly 67k per memory, about 30% less than single anchors.
